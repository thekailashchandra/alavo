# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Alavo to be a welcoming project for people who use it, self-host it, and contribute to it. We pledge to make participation free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction.
- Focus on the product and the change.
- Keep private user data out of issues, reviews, and examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or lock contributions and issues that break this standard, including warning or blocking a participant. Reports can be sent to the same contact used for security reports in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
